## Who we are

Jay and Michelle Miller. Married, Honolulu, both Army veterans, both age-groupers. We race the local calendar on Oahu plus a few things we get on a plane for. Jay's the faster engine in the house. Michelle's PRs are the story.

Jay's day job is Hawaii mortgages and has been for a long time. It has its own website. This isn't that website and it's never going to link there, because the minute a racing log starts trying to sell you something it stops being a racing log.

Michelle's background is hers to tell, and she'll tell the parts she wants to. On this site she's the person who put a marathon on a birthday, won her age group at a 10K, and ran a 2:12 half with Jay sitting on her shoulder.

## How it started

Jay did a few triathlons in 2005 and 2006 and didn't like them. In 2014 he talked Michelle into the Honolulu Triathlon, then the Tin Man two months later. We weren't really training. We hadn't decided this was a life, just a weekend with a timing chip. It didn't take.

In December 2021 Michelle watched the Honolulu Marathon and said we should run it the next year for Jay's fiftieth. He'd never wanted a marathon. The longest run in the house was the Great Aloha Run, 8.2 miles. We trained anyway. Hibiscus Half in May 2022 was the first half for both of us. The marathon that December was the birthday.

Then 2023 happened. The Honolulu Triathlon, raced for real this time. Michelle's half-marathon PR. Jay's first 70.3 in the lava at Honu. 112 miles around Oahu. The whole Marathon Readiness Series. The Honolulu Marathon again. We filmed every week of it. Then the decision to go the full distance, and the seasons since.

The order matters more than any one race. Olympic, then a 70.3 on a hard course, then a standalone marathon, then the full. We didn't skip a rung.

## The camera and the log

The YouTube channel is the camera. It's got the bike rack at 4:45 a.m., the finish chutes, the family visits, the blooper reel. One episode a week through 2023, then race films and year-in-review cuts after that.

This site is the written log. It exists because a race shouldn't only live inside a thumbnail. Three years from now, the sentence about what actually happened at mile 20 should still be findable, in words, with a date on it.

The two link to each other. They don't replace each other.

## The rules this site keeps

- **No made-up results.** If there's a time on a page, it's the official one. If there's no time, we didn't have it in front of us. We won't estimate a gun time to make a page look finished. Send us the official result and it goes up.
- **No coaching, no clinic, no medical advice.** We write what we did. We don't know your labs. Everything here is two people's notes, not a prescription.
- **No funnel.** Not for coaching, not for mortgages, not for anything. We don't sell electrolytes. If a piece of gear gets named, it's because we raced in it.
- **Specific beats inspiring.** A note about the dew point at a 5 a.m. start is worth more than a paragraph about believing in yourself. We'll take the dew point every time.

## Why bother writing it down

Because we started late, and we couldn't find the version of this written by people our age, in a place this hot, who weren't selling a plan. So we're writing it. If it gets one couple in their fifties to a start line upright, in the right shoes, with the argument about pace already settled, it did its job.

Two Honolulu age-groupers. Swim, bike, run, and the week around it.
