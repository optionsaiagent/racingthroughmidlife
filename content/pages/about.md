## Who we are

Jay and Michelle Miller. Married, Honolulu, both Army veterans, both age-groupers. We race the local calendar on Oahu and a few things we fly for. Jay is the faster engine in the house. Michelle's PRs are the story.

Jay has a day job in Hawaii mortgages and has had it for a long time. It has its own website. This is not that website, and it will never link to it, because the moment a racing log starts trying to sell you something it stops being a racing log.

Michelle's background is hers to tell, and she will tell the parts she wants to. On this site she is the person who put a marathon on a birthday, won her age group at a 10K, and ran a 2:12 half with Jay sitting on her shoulder.

## How it started

Jay did a few triathlons in 2005 and 2006 and did not like them. In 2014 he talked Michelle into the Honolulu Triathlon and then the Tin Man two months later. We were not really training. We had not decided this was a life, just a weekend with a timing chip. The bug had not landed.

In December 2021 Michelle watched the Honolulu Marathon and said we should run it the next year for Jay's fiftieth. He had never wanted a marathon. The longest run in the house was the Great Aloha Run, 8.2 miles. We trained anyway. Hibiscus Half in May 2022 was the first half for both of us. The marathon that December was the birthday.

Then 2023 happened: the Honolulu Triathlon raced seriously this time, Michelle's half-marathon PR, Jay's first 70.3 in the lava at Honu, 112 miles around Oahu, the whole Marathon Readiness Series, and the Honolulu Marathon again. We filmed every week of it. Then the decision to go the full distance, and the seasons that followed.

The order matters more than any single race. Olympic, then a 70.3 on a hard course, then a standalone marathon, then the full. Nobody skipped a rung.

## The camera and the log

The YouTube channel is the camera. It has the bike rack at 4:45 a.m., the finish chutes, the family visits, the blooper reel. One episode a week through 2023, then race films and year-in-review cuts after that.

This site is the written log. It exists because a race day should not live only inside a thumbnail. Three years from now, the sentence that says what actually happened at mile 20 should still be findable, in words, with a date on it.

They link to each other. They do not replace each other.

## The rules this site keeps

- **No invented results.** If there is a time on a page, it is the official one. If there is no time, we did not have it in front of us. We will not estimate a gun time to make a page look complete. Send the official result and it goes up.
- **No coaching, no clinic, no medical advice.** We write what we did. We do not know your labs. Everything here is two people's notes, not a prescription.
- **No funnel.** Not for coaching, not for mortgages, not for anything. We do not sell electrolytes. If a piece of gear is named, it is because we raced in it.
- **Specific over inspiring.** A note about the dew point at a 5 a.m. start is worth more than a paragraph about believing in yourself. We will take the dew point every time.

## Why write it down

Because we started late, and we could not find the version of this written by people our age, in a place this hot, who were not selling a plan. So we are writing it. If it helps one couple in their fifties get to a start line upright, with the right shoes on and the argument about pace already settled, it did its job.

Two Honolulu age-groupers. Swim, bike, run, and the week around it.
