Four Junes in the lava for me now: [6:48](/races/honu-70-3-2023), [5:26](/races/honu-70-3-2024), [5:44](/races/honu-70-3-2025), and 5:54:48 this year. Three for Michelle: [6:40](/races/honu-70-3-2024), [6:44](/races/honu-70-3-2025), and 6:59:03. The swim was the full distance again, and the wind on the road to Hawi was whatever it decided to be that morning, which is the only forecast for that road we've ever found reliable.

The card has the pieces: for me a 45-minute swim, 2:57 on the bike, and a 1:58 half; for Michelle a 42-minute swim, which was faster than mine, 3:27 on the bike, and a 2:34 run. Twelfth and fourteenth in the age groups. Not the fastest Honu for either of us, and after the [2025 season](/races/ironman-california-2025) we weren't really expecting it to be. What we were expecting was to be on the start line at Waikoloa at first light, and we were. This is the race we don't skip.

The next one is [Northern California](/races/northern-california-70-3-2026) in August.
