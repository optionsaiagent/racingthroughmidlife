Was it smart? No. We'd finished [IRONMAN Texas](/races/ironman-texas-2025) on April 26 and Honu was May 31. That's five weeks between a full and a half, and I'll say it plainly: we did it because Honu is the race we don't skip, not because anybody thought it was a good idea. I'm not sure anybody even asked.

6:44:44, tenth in the 45-49s. Four minutes off my time from the year before, with a full Ironman still in my legs, and honestly I expected worse. The bike held up better than I thought it would. The run was where April came to collect, all 2:25:06 of it, and I paid up. Jay 5:44:12, eleventh in the 50-54s, and his run told the same story as mine.

Full swim distance this year, all 1.2 miles of it, unlike the wind-shortened [2024 swim](/races/honu-70-3-2024). 41:53 on the card. I'd waited a year to find out what that swim actually felt like, and now I know, and I'd still take the full one.
