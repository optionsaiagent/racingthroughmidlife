The Honolulu Century is the big organized ride on the island. Late September, out of Kapiolani Park, up over the east side and along the windward coast and back, with turnaround points at 25, 50, 75, and 100 miles so it fits anyone.

## The ride

Out through Hawaii Kai and around the southeast tip, then north along the coast with the mountains on your left and the ocean on your right. It is the same windward coast that the [Kailua 10-Miler](/races/kailua-10-miler-2023) and the [Windward Half](/races/windward-half-2023) use, which means the same wind: one direction is a gift and the other is a tax.

The Hawaii Bicycling League has been running it for decades, and the rest stops are real rest stops with real food. After the [Dick Evans](/races/dick-evans-112-2023) a month earlier, where 112 miles was a race, the century was a long ride with support and a much better view.

## Where it sat

Late September 2023, with the [Runner's HI 20K](/races/runners-hi-20k-2023) and the [Waikiki swim](/races/waikiki-2-4-mile-swim-2023) earlier in the month and the [Hybrid Design 25K](/races/hybrid-design-25k-2023) the following weekend. The running series carried the marathon. The long rides carried the full-distance question. September was the month both of those were loudest.

## What we would tell a friend

If you ride on Oahu and you have not done this, do it. Pick the distance that matches where you are. The 25 and 50 are real rides with real hills, and nobody at the finish asks which turnaround you took. If you take the 100, start early, eat at every stop, and remember that the return leg is into the trades. The people who go out fast with the wind are the people you pass on the way home.
