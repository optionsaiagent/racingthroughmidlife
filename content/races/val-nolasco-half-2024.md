The rule for this race is to [run it as a check](/races/val-nolasco-half-2023), and I ran it as a check, or at least I'm fairly sure I did. Then I came through in 2:08:08, which is a half-marathon PR by four minutes over the [Hibiscus 2:12](/races/hibiscus-half-2023) that this whole site keeps bringing up. So was it a check or a race? It felt like a check to me, as I wasn't chasing anything and the legs just had more in them than the legs I brought to Hibiscus, and a month out from the marathon, finding that out was what the day was for. I'll take the PR as a bonus and not ask too many questions about it.

Jay ran 1:44:44, a few seconds off the year before, which is exactly what a check is supposed to look like, and mine looked like a PR instead. I was a little confused by that, but I did enjoy it. Four weeks later came the [marathon](/races/honolulu-marathon-2024), where both of us found out what the year had been for.
