The third race of March 2023, and the longest. A half marathon on the windward side, which is the side of the island that looks the way people imagine Hawaii looks.

## The race

Green mountains, wet air, and a course that is beautiful in the way that distracts you from the fact that the humidity on the windward side is higher than in town. By mile nine every half marathon here is a heat race. This one is a heat race with a view.

## Where it sat in the year

March 2023 had a 10K, another 10K, and this half in it, in consecutive weeks. That was the spring build toward the [Hapalua](/races/hapalua-half-2023) in April and the [Honolulu Triathlon](/races/honolulu-triathlon-2014-and-again) in May. Three races in a month is a lot. It worked because only the half was run at anything like a real effort, and even that was run as a long run with a bib, not a goal.

## What we would tell a friend

Do not let the scenery set your pace. The windward side is gorgeous and it is wetter than town, and the wet is what gets you. Go out easy, take every aid station, and enjoy the mountains from a heart rate you can sustain. The people who race the first half of a windward half are the people walking the second half of it.
