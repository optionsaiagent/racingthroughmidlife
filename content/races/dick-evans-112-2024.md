Second lap of the island for me, and Michelle wasn't on the start list this year, so this is one of the few pages here that's just one of us. 6:09:26 for 112 miles, which is a long time to be on a bike in August on this island, in a category the race calls Golden Masters. That's a polite way of saying 50 to 59, and we've decided to take it as a compliment.

We don't have an official 2023 result for this race. It wasn't posted on the timing site the 2024 and 2025 editions use, and we're not going to make one up. [The 2023 page](/races/dick-evans-112-2023) has the story from the first lap; this page has the first official time, forty-seventh in the category with bib 85.
