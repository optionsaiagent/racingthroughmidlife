If you are running the Honolulu Marathon in December, this is where the build starts having a bib on it.

## The series

The Marathon Readiness Series is five races between August and November, each one longer than the last: 15K, 20K, 25K, 30K, then a half marathon a month out from the marathon. Look at that and you are looking at the long-run progression of every marathon plan on the internet, except with aid stations and other people. We [let the calendar be the plan](/lessons/let-the-calendar-be-the-plan) in 2023 and it worked.

The 15K is race one. About 9.3 miles, in August, which means it is hot from the first mile and honest about it.

## The week

The first race of the series sat in the middle of August, a week after a training week with hill sprints, a ridge ride, and a Koko Head hike in it, and two weeks before [112 miles around the island](/races/dick-evans-112-2023). That is the balance of a fall here: the running series carries the marathon build, and the long rides carry the full-distance question that was starting to be asked out loud.

## What we would tell a friend

Run the 15K as a long run with a bib. Do not race it. It is August, the marathon is four months away, and the series has four more races in it. The information you want from this one is simple: how does 9 miles feel in the heat with a number on, and what did you eat that worked. That is all. Save the racing for December.
