This is the race the whole site is about, in the sense that every page before it is a rung on the ladder that ends here.

## How the ladder got here

Honolulu Triathlon, [raced seriously](/races/honolulu-triathlon-2014-and-again), May 2023. A 70.3 [in the lava](/races/honu-70-3-2023), June 2023. [112 miles around Oahu](/races/dick-evans-112-2023), August 2023. [2.4 miles off Waikiki](/races/waikiki-2-4-mile-swim-2023), September 2023. A [marathon](/races/honolulu-marathon-2023) with a readiness series behind it, December 2023. Then a year and change of the training that a full distance requires, [funded with sleep](/lessons/sleep-funds-the-season), most of it starting at 5 a.m. in a garage or on a road before the sun.

None of it was skipped. That is the only training advice on this page.

## The race

IRONMAN Texas is in The Woodlands, north of Houston, in late April. A 2.4-mile swim in a lake, a 112-mile bike on closed roads that are flat and fast and windy, and a marathon on a course that loops through the town past the same spectators several times. It is a long way from Honolulu, which means a bike box, a long flight, and every lesson in [Race week is a logistics problem](/lessons/race-week-is-a-logistics-problem).

The one thing Texas had in common with home was the heat. April in The Woodlands can be warm and humid, and the run starts in the afternoon. The [fourth sport](/notes/heat-is-the-fourth-sport) came along for the trip.
