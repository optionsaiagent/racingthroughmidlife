The 30K is the longest race in the readiness series, eighteen and a half miles, and in October of 2022 it was the longest either of us had ever run, by a lot. Not by a mile or two. By a lot. The marathon was seven weeks out, and there's a difference between a long run you plan and a distance with a start line and a clock on it, and we found that out somewhere in the back half of this one. I came in at 3:01:11. Michelle came in at 4:08:27. We were both upright at the finish, which was the goal, even if neither of us had said so out loud.

A year later we'd learned to [run this one as a rehearsal](/races/boca-hawaii-30k-2023) rather than a race: same distance, same October, different attitude. In 2022 we didn't know the difference yet. We ran it the way you run something when you aren't sure you can finish it, which is to say a little scared and a little too hard. The marathon seven weeks later was survivable anyway. That's the thing about the series. It works even when you're doing it wrong, and for me, doing it wrong looked like 9:43 a mile for eighteen and a half miles.
