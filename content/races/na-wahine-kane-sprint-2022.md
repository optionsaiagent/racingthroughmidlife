Over the summer of 2022 we started working a little cycling and swimming into the week, mostly to break up the running and give our joints a rest, and at the end of July we signed up for this. It was the first year the Na Wahine sprint was open to men, and it was short: a 500-meter swim, a 12-mile bike, and a 5K run.

It was a lot of fun. That's the honest report, and it's also why it mattered, because after it we kept the swim and the bike in the weekly rotation for variety, and a year later the [Honolulu Triathlon](/races/honolulu-triathlon-2014-and-again) and [Honu](/races/honu-70-3-2023) were on the calendar. We don't have the official times in front of us for this one, and the next year Jay came back and did the Kane race [on his own](/races/na-wahine-kane-sprint-2023).
