This is the flat town 10K we run in early March to find out where the legs are, and in 2024 the answer was two minutes faster than [2023](/races/kings-runner-10k-2023) for me, 44:36 by the watch. Michelle ran 57:18. A year later on this course I'd run [42:53](/races/kings-runner-10k-2025), so this was the middle step.

The official sheet for 2024 isn't posted anywhere we can read, so these are from our watches. The next race was the [Windward Half](/races/windward-half-2024) three weeks later.
