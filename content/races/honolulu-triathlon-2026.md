We've done this race in [2014](/races/honolulu-triathlon-2014-and-again), 2023, [2024](/races/honolulu-triathlon-2024), [2025](/races/honolulu-triathlon-2025), and now 2026, and it's the best measuring stick we have, because the course doesn't change and it always sits two weeks before Honu. For me it was 2:28:38, seventh in the 50-54s, about two minutes off last year. Michelle went 2:52:43, sixth in hers, five minutes off the year she won it.

The swim was quicker than 2025 for both of us and the bike was within a minute, so the difference was the run, and the run is where a lighter year shows first, which is fine with us. The point of 2026 was to get to the start line of [Honu](/races/honu-70-3-2026) healthy, and two weeks later we did. We didn't write this one up at the time, so the card is the report.
