After a 70.3, the month of June was family visits and hikes and a slow return to running. July was about getting some speed back, and an 8K is exactly the distance for that.

## The race

Lanikai is a small residential loop on the windward side, the neighborhood with the beach that ends up on postcards. An 8K there is a fast, flat loop that is over before the heat fully arrives, which in July means it is merely humid rather than punishing.

## Why an 8K

Because after six weeks of long-course training and a race in the lava, the legs had forgotten what fast felt like. The same week had sprints in it, actual short hard efforts, for the first time since the spring. An 8K with a bib was the way to put that speed somewhere.

Five miles is also short enough to race without wrecking the week after. That mattered, because the [Na Wahine & Kane sprint triathlon](/races/na-wahine-kane-sprint-2023) was the following weekend and the new triathlon bike had just arrived.

## What we would tell a friend

After a long-course race, do not go straight back into long. Go short for a few weeks. An 8K or a 10K with real effort reminds the legs that they have gears, and it makes the long stuff feel easier when it comes back. Also, Lanikai is worth the drive over the Pali just for the loop.
