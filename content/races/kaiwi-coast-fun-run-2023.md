The Kaiwi coast is the stretch of the southeast shore between Sandy Beach and Makapuu, with the ocean on one side and lava cliffs on the other. It is one of the most beautiful pieces of road on the island. A fun run on it in November is exactly what it sounds like.

## Why a fun run in a taper

Three weeks before the marathon, the plan is about staying sharp and staying rested, and a short run with a bib and a view does both. No time goal. No pressure. A morning out with other people who run, on a road that is usually full of cars, and then breakfast.

We are not going to pretend this was a race. It was a reason to get up and run somewhere nice. Those are worth having in a season, especially in the weeks when the training is winding down and the temptation is to do nothing at all or to do too much.

## The week

The same week was week two of Zwift Academy in the garage, which is the [indoor cycling](/notes/the-trainer-in-the-garage) program that had taken over the weekday bike by then. Fun run on the weekend, structured trainer sessions during the week, marathon in three weeks. That was November.

## What we would tell a friend

Keep one fun run in every season. Not every race has to mean something. A short run on the Kaiwi coast with a number on and nothing on the line is a reminder of why any of this is worth doing at 5 a.m. the rest of the year.
