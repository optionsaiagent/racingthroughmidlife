A 10K is the shortest distance we race and the one that tells the most uncomfortable truth. There is nowhere to hide in six miles. The pace you can hold is the pace you have.

## The race

A town 10K in early March. Flat, early, well organized. The kind of race where the whole thing is over before the heat has really started, which in March means it is merely warm.

## Why it was there

After a winter of long, slow miles and a January that was mostly about recovery, we wanted a number. Not a goal-race number. A reference. What do the legs do when asked to go faster than marathon pace for forty-some minutes? The answer tells you where the spring is starting from.

It also set up the rest of March: the [Ku'ikahi 10K](/races/kuikahi-10k-2023) two weeks later, where Michelle won her age group, and the [Windward Half](/races/windward-half-2023) the week after that.

## What we would tell a friend

Run a 10K early in the year, not to race it, but to know. The number is not a judgment. It is a starting point, and the whole year is about moving it. And a 10K done at real effort costs less than a half done at real effort, so it is the cheap way to get the information.
