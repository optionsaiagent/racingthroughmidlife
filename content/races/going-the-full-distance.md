Training for a full Ironman in Honolulu is a heat problem, a marriage problem, and a calendar problem. This is the page about the decision, not the race. The race gets its own page, with the official result on it when we have it in front of us.

## The order

We did not start with the full distance. We would tell anyone not to. The ladder we actually climbed looks like this:

1. An Olympic-distance triathlon, [raced properly](/races/honolulu-triathlon-2014-and-again) after a 2014 attempt without training.
2. A 70.3 on a [hard course](/races/honu-70-3-2023), in the lava, in June, on purpose. If the half was going to be survivable anywhere, it should be survivable there.
3. A [standalone marathon](/races/honolulu-marathon-2023), then another one, with a whole readiness series in front of it.
4. The Ironman bike distance [alone](/races/dick-evans-112-2023), around the island, in August.
5. The Ironman swim distance [alone](/races/waikiki-2-4-mile-swim-2023), in the ocean off Waikiki.

Only after every one of those was done, and not theoretical, did the full distance become a thing we were allowed to talk about.

## What it costs at fifty

The full distance is not a personality. It is a season you have to fund with sleep.

Every long-course season since has been paid for the same way: earlier bedtimes, fewer evenings, a social calendar that got thin for a few months. The long rides start at 5 a.m. because of the heat and take most of a Saturday. The long runs take most of a Sunday. The trainer in the garage takes the weekday hours that used to belong to something else.

It is a heat problem because the training has to happen in Honolulu, where every long session is a heat session and the recovery from it takes longer than the pace suggests. It is a marriage problem because two people in one house cannot both be in a full-distance build without the [whose day is it](/lessons/whose-day-is-it) rule doing real work every single weekend. It is a calendar problem because the season is long, the job does not care, and the [plan is written in pencil](/lessons/the-plan-is-written-in-pencil).

## The week around it

The highlight reel is the swim start and the finish chute. The week is a bike box, a spreadsheet, a grocery list of food that has already been tested in the heat, and a lot of standing in lines. We wrote it all down in [Race week is a logistics problem](/lessons/race-week-is-a-logistics-problem), because nobody had written it down for people our age when we needed it.

## What we would tell a friend

Do the Olympic first. Do a 70.3 on a course that scares you a little. Run a marathon on its own, without a swim and a bike in front of it, and find out what mile 22 feels like when that is the only thing you did that day. Ride the bike distance alone. Swim the swim distance alone.

Then, if you still want it, go the full distance. And fund it with sleep.
