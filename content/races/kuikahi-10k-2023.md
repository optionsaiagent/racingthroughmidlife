We are two age-groupers and we have exactly one age-group win in the house. This is it.

## The context

Four weeks before this race, Michelle hurt a rib. We are not going to diagnose it here. What it did to training was simple: breathing is most of running, and a rib makes breathing a negotiation. The plan for March had been written in pencil and a lot of it got erased.

She raced anyway, at a distance short enough not to be foolish, on a day when the rib allowed it.

## The result

First in her age group. That is the only result on this site that we can state as fact from our own memory without a timing page in front of us, because you do not forget the first time your wife wins something with a number on it. When the official time is in hand, it will go next to it.

## What it says about training

A race is not the last three weeks. It is the two years before them. Michelle had a marathon in December, a half in January, and a consistent year behind that. A month of disrupted training did not erase it. That is the part worth remembering at fifty: the base is durable. A bad month is a bad month, not a lost year.
