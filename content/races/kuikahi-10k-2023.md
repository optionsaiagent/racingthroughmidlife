This was the first age-group win in the house, and it was Michelle's.

## What was going on

Four weeks before this race Michelle hurt a rib. We're not going to diagnose it here. What it did to training is simple: breathing is most of running, and a rib turns breathing into a negotiation. The plan for March had been written in pencil and a lot of it got erased.

She raced anyway, at a distance short enough not to be dumb about it, on a day the rib allowed.

## The result

First in her age group. This race wasn't timed by Timeline Hawaii, so we don't have the official time in front of us yet, but you don't forget the first time your wife wins something with a number on it. When the official time turns up it goes right next to it.

## What it says about training

A race isn't the last three weeks. It's the two years before them. Michelle had a marathon in December, a half in January, and a consistent year behind that. A month of messed-up training didn't erase it. That's worth remembering at fifty: the base is durable. A bad month is a bad month, not a lost year.
