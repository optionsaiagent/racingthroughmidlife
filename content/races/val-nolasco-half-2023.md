Race five of five. A half marathon four weeks before the Honolulu Marathon, which is a distance and a date that practically beg you to make a mistake.

The mistake is racing it. The legs are as fit as they'll be all year, the half is a distance where a PR is on the table, and the urge to find out is enormous. But a raced half a month before a marathon is a withdrawal from an account that needs to be full in December.

So we treated it as a check. The last long run with a bib, at marathon effort, with the marathon breakfast and the marathon shoes. Everything the [30K](/races/boca-hawaii-30k-2023) had checked, checked once more, closer to the day.

Four weeks out is where the taper starts, and this race is the door into it. Get the information, then let the volume come down. Nothing you do in the last four weeks will make you fitter, and plenty of what you could do would make you more tired.

Zwift Academy was running in the garage the same month, so by November the fall had a shape: the running series carried the marathon build, the [trainer](/notes/the-trainer-in-the-garage) carried the weekday bike, and the weekends went to whatever was longest. The next bib was the [Kaiwi Coast Fun Run](/races/kaiwi-coast-fun-run-2023) the following week, and that one wasn't allowed to count for anything.
