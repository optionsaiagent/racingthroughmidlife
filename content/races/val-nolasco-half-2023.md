Race five of five. A half marathon four weeks before the Honolulu Marathon, which is a distance and a timing that invite a mistake.

## The mistake

The mistake is racing it. The legs are as fit as they will be all year. The half is a distance where a PR is possible. The temptation to find out is enormous. And a raced half marathon a month before a marathon is a withdrawal from an account that needs to be full in December.

So this was a check, not a race. The last long run with a bib, at marathon effort, with the marathon breakfast and the marathon shoes. Everything the [30K](/races/boca-hawaii-30k-2023) had checked, checked once more, closer to the day.

## The week

Zwift Academy was running in the garage the same month, and the [Kaiwi Coast Fun Run](/races/kaiwi-coast-fun-run-2023) was the following week. By November the pattern of the fall was set: the running series carried the marathon build, the [trainer](/notes/the-trainer-in-the-garage) carried the weekday bike, and the weekends were for whatever was longest.

## What we would tell a friend

Four weeks out is where the taper starts, and the Val Nolasco is the door into it. Run it, get the information, and then let the volume come down. The fitness is built. The next four weeks are for arriving at the start line rested, and nothing you do in them will make you fitter. Plenty of what you could do would make you more tired.
