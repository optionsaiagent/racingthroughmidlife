I ran 1:34:34 this year, sixth in the 45-49s, and those weren't last year's legs. I'm fine with that, or at least most of me is fine with that, and the rest of me will get over it. 2026 wasn't built for a fast February. It was built around [Honu in May](/races/honu-70-3-2026) and a [mainland 70.3 in August](/races/northern-california-70-3-2026), and a quick 10-miler on the windward side just wasn't on the list.

The wind was in our faces again, the way it is every February out there, and it wasn't the [1:13 of 2025](/races/kailua-10-miler-2025) for Jay either. He ran 1:19:19 for third in the 50-54s, which makes four years in a row on the age-group podium here, so he was slower than last year and still on the podium, which is a very Jay thing to do.
