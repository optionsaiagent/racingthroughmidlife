There is a 10K in Hawaii Kai on the morning of January 1. It starts at sunrise, which on this island is a reasonable hour, and it is full of people who decided the year should start with a run rather than a hangover.

## Why start the year here

Three weeks after the [Honolulu Marathon](/races/honolulu-marathon-2023), the legs were recovered and the routine was not. A [January](/notes/january-the-five-pounds-and-the-groove) always needs a reason to get the alarm back, and a bib on the first morning of the year is a good reason. It is not a race to race. It is a race to attend.

Hawaii Kai is the east end of the marathon course, the flat, exposed stretch that hurts in December. On New Year's morning it is a different place: cool, quiet, and short.

## What we would tell a friend

If you live somewhere with a New Year's Day race, do it. Not for the time. For the fact that the year starts with the routine already running. The people at that start line are not the fittest people on the island. They are the ones who will still be running in March, because they never stopped.

The next thing on the calendar was a [trail run out to Kaena Point](/races/kaena-point-10-mile-2024), which is about as different from a Hawaii Kai 10K as two runs on one island can be.
