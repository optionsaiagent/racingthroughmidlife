We did something crazy in 2024 besides the [70.3 in the wind](/races/honu-70-3-2024). The Spartan Beast is the long one, 20 kilometers with obstacles, and the 2024 edition was at Mokuleia on the North Shore, a week after the [15K](/races/norman-tamanaha-15k-2024) and a week before I rode [around the island](/races/dick-evans-112-2024). Our watches say a little under four hours and forty-three minutes from start to finish, and my memory says about five hours, which tells you what the last hour felt like.

It's a 12-mile hike up and down North Shore hills with walls, ropes, carries, and mud between the hills. We stayed together the whole way. Somewhere in there I went over an eight-foot wall and hurt my left shoulder, and I'm still nursing that injury two years later. It hasn't healed all the way yet. That's a story for another page.

The Beast was one third of a Trifecta, which is the three Spartan distances in one calendar year. We got the other two in Nashville in October, the [Super](/races/spartan-super-nashville-2024) and the [Sprint](/races/spartan-sprint-nashville-2024), after Hurricane Helene canceled the race we'd actually flown out for.

No timing sheet on this one, so the times are from our watches.
