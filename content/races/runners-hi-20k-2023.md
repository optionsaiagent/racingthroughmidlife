Race two of five. A 20K, about 12.4 miles, which is nearly a half marathon in a month when the whole island is a sauna.

## September

People who do not live here assume summer is the hot part. It is, but the worst of it is late. September is when the trade winds get lazy and the dew point sits in the 70s and a run that starts at 5:30 a.m. is already humid before the first mile. Every race in the readiness series is a heat race, and this one is the hottest of them.

## The week

The 20K opened a September that also had the [Waikiki 2.4-mile swim](/races/waikiki-2-4-mile-swim-2023), the [Honolulu Century Ride](/races/honolulu-century-ride-2023), and the start of [zone 2 training](/notes/zone-2-in-a-sauna) in it. That is a lot of month. It worked because the 20K was a long run with a bib, not a race, and the long ride and the long swim were spread across different weekends.

## What we would tell a friend

At 20K the fueling starts to matter in a way it did not at 15K. This is the race in the series where you should be eating on the run, testing what will be in your pockets in December, and drinking at every station whether you feel like it or not. Twelve miles in September heat is a rehearsal for the marathon's exposed stretch toward Hawaii Kai, which is also flat, also hot, and also where people's days fall apart.
