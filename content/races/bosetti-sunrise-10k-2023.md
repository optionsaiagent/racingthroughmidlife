The [first video of the vlog year](/notes/january-the-five-pounds-and-the-groove) is about losing the holiday weight, which tells you what the second half of December looked like at our house once the marathon was done. This race is what we did on the morning of the first day of the year, before the video and before the diet.

A 10K in Hawaii Kai at sunrise on January 1 is a nice idea and not a serious race, and we treated it about that way. For me, it went better than it had any right to: 47:39, third in the 50-54s, three weeks after the first marathon, which nobody expected, least of all me. Michelle ran 1:14:01, fifth in her age group, and had exactly zero interest in racing a 10K three weeks after 26.2. Honestly, hers was the correct attitude for January 1, and mine was the fluke. Then we went home and started on the holiday weight.
