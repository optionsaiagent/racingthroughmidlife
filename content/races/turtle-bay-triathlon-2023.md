The last triathlon of 2023, two weeks before the marathon, on the North Shore at Turtle Bay. By late November the winter swells have started arriving on that side of the island, which makes a swim leg there a different proposition than one inside the reef at Ala Moana in May.

## Where it sat

Two weeks out from a marathon is not when most plans put a triathlon. But the swim and the bike are not the marathon's legs, a short course is a short course, and after a year that started with the [Honolulu Tri](/races/honolulu-triathlon-2014-and-again), went through the lava at [Honu](/races/honu-70-3-2023) and a [sprint](/races/na-wahine-kane-sprint-2023) in July, it felt right to close the triathlon year with a bib on before the running year closed with its own.

It was done easy. Taper rules applied. The [Val Nolasco half](/races/val-nolasco-half-2023) two weeks earlier had been the last hard effort, and nothing at Turtle Bay was allowed to change that.

## The race

A short-course triathlon with an adventure-race option, on the resort grounds and the North Shore roads around them. The video shows the day better than a paragraph would. Watch it.

## What we would tell a friend

If you race triathlon on Oahu, the year has a shape: the Honolulu Tri in May, sprints through the summer, and a late-season race on the North Shore when the water has changed. Ending on Turtle Bay is a nice way to remember that the sport is supposed to be fun, right before a marathon reminds you that it is also supposed to be hard.
