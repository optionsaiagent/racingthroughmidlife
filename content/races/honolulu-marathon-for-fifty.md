Jay did not want a marathon. Not in the way people say they do not want one and then sign up anyway. He did not like running, he had never run past eight miles, and he had built a fairly comfortable identity around not being a marathon person.

Then Michelle stood at the 2021 finish and said we should do it next year, for his fiftieth. There was no good argument against a birthday.

## The year

The training was not dramatic. It was consistent, which at this age is the only kind that works. Dawn runs before the day job. The long run built through the spring until a [half marathon at Hibiscus](/races/hibiscus-half-2022) in May proved 13.1 was possible. Then the long run built past that through a Honolulu summer, which is the part of this story that deserves more sympathy than it gets. Long runs in August here start in the dark and end in a different climate.

We signed up when the half was done. Not before. The [long run had to exist first](/lessons/dont-sign-up-before-the-long-run).

## The race

The Honolulu Marathon starts at 5 a.m. at Ala Moana, in the dark, with fireworks. It goes through Waikiki, up and around Diamond Head, out the long flat exposed stretch toward Hawaii Kai, back the same way, and over Diamond Head again to finish at Kapiolani Park. There is no cutoff time, which is one of the reasons it is the right first marathon for people who are not fast. Nobody sweeps you. You finish when you finish.

The second Diamond Head, at mile 24 or so, is where the race tells you the truth about your training. The flat part before it, in the sun, is where it tells you the truth about your fueling.

We finished. We are not going to put a time on this page that we do not have in front of us, and this race was before the log. When the official result is in hand it goes here.

## What it changed

Everything after this exists because of this. Not because the marathon was the goal. Because it proved that a long day was survivable at fifty, with the right shoes, the right food, and a plan written in pencil.

Midlife is not a sports car, for us. It turned out to be this.

A year later we ran it [again](/races/honolulu-marathon-2023), with a camera.
