A pentathlon is five events in a day. The Oahu Pentathlon is the local version, and in 2025 it was one of the days we filmed rather than wrote about at the time.

## The day

The video is the report for this one. We are not going to reconstruct the events from memory and put them on a page as though we had notes, because we did not keep notes that day and this site does not guess. Watch it. It is one of the most-watched things on the channel, which tells us that multi-event days with a camera on them are what people want to see.

## Why it is on the calendar

Because after a full-distance season, a day with five different events in it is a reminder that the sport does not have to be one long thing. It can be several short things, with other people, in a place you already live. The years that are not full-distance years need days like this in them, or the alarm at 5 a.m. stops making sense.

When the official results are in hand, they go here. When we have written the day down properly, that goes here too.
