A pentathlon is five events in one day. The Oahu Pentathlon is the local version, and in 2025 it was a day we filmed instead of writing down.

## The one number I have

Fifteen hours and twenty-two minutes. We started at 6 in the morning and finished at 9:22 at night, together. That's longer than either of our full Ironmans, and it's the longest day with a bib on that either of us has had. The timing company's results for this race live on a live-tracking map that doesn't publish a finish list, so the time on the card is ours, from our own clock, not from a timing sheet. It's the only number on this site that isn't.

## The rest

The video is the report. We didn't keep notes that day, and I'm not going to rebuild five events from memory and put them on a page like we did. Watch it. It's one of the most-watched things on the channel, which tells me people would rather see a multi-event day with a camera on it than read about one. Fair.

I'm a routine person. A day with five different events in it, with other people around, with a camera going, is the opposite of a routine, and I'm not going to pretend I went into it calm. Was it worth it? After a full-distance season, a day like this is a reminder that the sport doesn't have to be one long thing. It can be several short things, with other people, in a place you already live. Fifteen hours of short things, it turns out. Six in the morning to 9:22 at night.
