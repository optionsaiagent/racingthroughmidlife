Race three of five. About 15.5 miles, which is the first time since the previous December's marathon that either of us ran past the half-marathon distance with a number on.

## Why 25K matters

The gap between a half marathon and a marathon is where most first-timers get lost. Thirteen miles feels like an accomplishment. Twenty-six feels like a rumor. The 25K and the [30K](/races/boca-hawaii-30k-2023) after it are the two races in the series that close that gap, and they are the reason the series works. By November the long run has been past 15 and past 18 with aid stations, and December is a step, not a leap.

## The week

The 25K came a week after the [Honolulu Century](/races/honolulu-century-ride-2023), which was 100 miles on the bike. That is a long ride and a long run on consecutive weekends, and the second one was run accordingly: easy, fueled, and finished with something left. This is the part of the fall where the [plan is in pencil](/lessons/the-plan-is-written-in-pencil) and the [sleep budget](/lessons/sleep-funds-the-season) is the thing that decides whether the week works.

## What we would tell a friend

From the 25K on, treat every series race as a marathon rehearsal. Same breakfast you will eat in December. Same shoes. Same pace strategy: first third boring, middle third steady, last third honest. If you race the 25K in late September, you will have a great result and a bad October. Nobody needs a great 25K.
