Three weeks after a marathon, a half marathon is either a dumb idea or a very specific good one. I'm going to say ours was the second, and I'm mostly sure of that.

The Honolulu Marathon was December 11. Then the holidays did what the holidays do to a routine person, which is take the routine away and give nothing back. The [first week of January](/notes/january-the-five-pounds-and-the-groove) was about getting the routine back, not about fitness. The Akahai half was on the calendar mostly so the long run would have a reason to keep existing while everything else recovered.

So we didn't race it. We ran it. No time goal, every aid station, and the whole idea was to finish feeling like there was more in the tank. Isn't that how you want to feel three weeks after a marathon? The fitness was still there. The recovery wasn't. Racing on the first without the second is how you spend the spring on the couch, and I've got a couch, I don't need more time on it.

The same week had a Kuliouou hike in it. That's a ridge trail on the east side that goes up and then keeps going up, and the way down is a leg workout I underestimate every single time. Hikes count as training here in a way they don't on the mainland. The climbs are real and so is the heat.

Both are in the video. Week three of the vlog year, routine back, no hole in the calendar.
