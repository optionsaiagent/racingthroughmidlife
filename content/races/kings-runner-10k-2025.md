A flat town 10K in early March, seven weeks before [IRONMAN Texas](/races/ironman-texas-2025), and the fastest 10K either of us has run with a number on. For me it was 42:53, fourth in the 50-54s, and I usually run this race as a check on where the legs are after a winter of long slow miles. That winter the legs were somewhere I hadn't been before. Michelle ran 53:52 for tenth in her age group, and it was the last race she ran as a 40-44 before the birthday moved her up.

We didn't film this one and didn't write it up at the time, so the card is the report. The next race was the [Windward Half](/races/windward-half-2025) three weeks later, where I won the age group and Michelle went under two hours for the first time.
