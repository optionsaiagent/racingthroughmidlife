The internet will tell you to sign up first so you can't back out. Sometimes that works, and sometimes it puts somebody who's never run 13 miles into a 26.2 they'll remember as a medical event. We did it the other way around, and it's the only reason the marathon happened at all.

## The date and the work are two different things

Michelle watched the 2021 Honolulu Marathon and said we should run it the next year for Jay's fiftieth. That was the date, and it did its job, because it turned a marathon into something that existed in the future instead of something Jay had decided he'd never want.

The date wasn't the plan, though. The longest run in the house at that point was 8.2 miles, so a marathon wasn't real yet. It got real in stages: a long run that went past ten, a half marathon at Hibiscus in May, long runs that went past the half, and then December. We paid for the marathon when the half was done and not before. If the half had gone badly the marathon would've slid to the next year and we'd have been fine with that, since nobody keeps score on a fiftieth birthday except the guy having it.

## Bridges, not leaps

Every distance since then has followed the same shape. The half was the bridge to the marathon, the Olympic tri in May 2023 was the bridge to the 70.3 two weeks later, the 112-mile Dick Evans ride in August was the bridge to the full-distance bike leg, and the standalone marathon was the bridge to the run at the end of the full. A bridge is a race you can already picture finishing, and it tells you the truth about the next one before you've paid for it.

## Why this matters more after 45

At 30 you can bluff a distance, you'll be sore and you'll finish. In your forties and fifties the bluff has consequences that last weeks. A body that hasn't done the long run doesn't have the tendons, the eating habits, or the pacing sense to survive a bad day at a long distance, and the bib doesn't give you any of that. The long run does, which is why ours got past ten miles, and then past the half, before the marathon got paid for.
