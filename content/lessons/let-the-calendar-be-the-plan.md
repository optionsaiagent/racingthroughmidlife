We are not coaches and we do not write training plans. But we had to get from a hot August to a marathon in December, and we noticed the city had already written the plan for us.

## The series

The Honolulu Marathon Readiness Series is five races, each one longer than the last, spaced about a month apart from August to November:

| # | Race | Distance | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Norman Tamanaha 15K](/races/norman-tamanaha-15k-2023) | 15K | August |
| 2 | [Runner's HI 20K](/races/runners-hi-20k-2023) | 20K | September |
| 3 | [Hybrid Design 25K](/races/hybrid-design-25k-2023) | 25K | Late September or early October |
| 4 | [BOCA Hawaii 30K](/races/boca-hawaii-30k-2023) | 30K | October |
| 5 | [Val Nolasco Half](/races/val-nolasco-half-2023) | 13.1 mi | November |

Look at that and you are looking at the long-run progression every marathon plan on the internet would give you, except this one has aid stations, a start line, and other people.

In 2023 we ran all five. Between them we did the long rides, the Waikiki swim, the Honolulu Century, and the first ride over the Pali. The marathon in December did not feel like a leap. It felt like the sixth race in a series.

## What a bib does that a plan does not

A long run on your own calendar can move. It can move to Sunday, then to next week, then to the week after that because family is visiting. A long run with a bib on it does not move. You paid for it, other people are going, and the alarm is set.

At fifty, the discipline is not the hard part. The scheduling is. The series takes the scheduling away.

## The one adjustment

The 30K in October is the trap. It is long enough to feel like an accomplishment and it is only six weeks from the marathon. Run it at marathon effort, not 30K effort. Same with the Val Nolasco half in November. Get the information: how the fueling held, how the legs felt at mile ten, what the heat did after 8 a.m. Then go home and rest.

If your town has something like this, and most of them have something, let it be the plan. You will spend less time inventing a schedule and more time running the one that exists.
