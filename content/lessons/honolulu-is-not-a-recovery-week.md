Every year we watch it happen. Someone flies in from a cool place with a great training block behind them, lands on a Thursday, spends Friday at the beach, and races Sunday in conditions their body has never met.

The finish line photos still look good. The middle of the race did not.

## The island is a race condition, not a backdrop

The Honolulu Triathlon, the Great Aloha Run, the Hapalua, Hibiscus, the Honolulu Marathon: these are real races run by real people who trained for them. The course is not gentle because there are palm trees on it. The Honolulu Marathon starts at 5 a.m. specifically so that the front of the field gets some of the run done before the sun does its job. The back of the field does not get that gift.

What surprises visitors is not the heat. Most people have run in 85 degrees. It is the humidity, which means sweat does not evaporate, which means the cooling you are used to does not happen, which means your heart rate drifts up at a pace that felt easy at home. You are not out of shape. You are in a different physics.

## What we tell friends who are flying in

Get here early. Not a week early, most people cannot. But early enough to run twice at the hour the race starts. Your body learns the temperature faster than you expect, but it does not learn it from a hotel room.

Drink and salt in the days before, on real food. Not because we sell anything. Because the first hour of a hot race is decided by the 48 hours before it, and you cannot catch up mid-race.

Then race the first third easier than you think you should. Every race here is a heat race by the second half. The people who look strong at the end are the ones who were bored at the start.

## The pretty course problem

Diamond Head is a climb. The Kalanianaole out-and-back to Hawaii Kai is exposed for miles. The Honu bike course goes to Hawi through lava fields where the wind has an opinion. It is all beautiful. None of it is a recovery week.

Come race here. We would love to see you. Just bring the respect you would bring to a hard course anywhere else, and then add the dew point.
