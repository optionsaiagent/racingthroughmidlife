The first video we ever posted as a weekly log was about losing five holiday pounds. Not an inspiring opening. An honest one.

## Where we were

The Honolulu Marathon was December 11, 2022. Jay's fiftieth birthday race, the one he never wanted, done. Then Christmas, then New Year's, then that specific kind of tired you get from finishing a big thing and immediately stopping.

The first week of January wasn't a training week. It was a week of getting the routine back. The alarm, the dark, the shoes by the door. The five pounds were real but they weren't the point. The groove was the point. The weight follows the groove. It doesn't lead it.

## What "back in the groove" actually looked like

Short, easy, early. Nothing that'd make anybody's highlight reel. A run that was mostly about being outside before the sun. A swim that was mostly about remembering how to swim. The plan for the year had a half marathon in it three weeks out, the [Akahai](/races/akahai-half-2023), and that wasn't a goal race. It was a reason to keep the long run alive through the holiday hangover.

## Why this note is here

Because every year has a January, every January feels like starting over, and the temptation is to make it dramatic. Big goals, big block, big first week. We've watched that fail in other people and in ourselves.

The version that works is smaller. Get the alarm back. Get the first easy week done. Let the five pounds sort themselves out once the routine does. January's for finding the groove, not proving anything.
