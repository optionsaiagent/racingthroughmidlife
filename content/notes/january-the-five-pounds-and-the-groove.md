The first video we ever posted as a weekly log was about losing five holiday pounds. That is not an inspiring opening. It is an honest one.

## Where we were

The Honolulu Marathon was December 11, 2022. Jay's fiftieth birthday race, the one he never wanted, done. Then Christmas, then New Year's, then the specific kind of tired that comes from finishing a big thing and immediately stopping.

The first week of January was not a training week. It was a week of getting the routine back: the alarm, the dawn, the shoes by the door. The five pounds were real but they were not the point. The groove was the point. The weight follows the groove. It does not lead it.

## What "back into the groove" looked like

Short, easy, early. Nothing that would show up on anyone's highlight reel. A run that was mostly about being outside before the sun. A swim that was mostly about remembering how to swim. The plan for the year had a half marathon in it in three weeks, the [Akahai](/races/akahai-half-2023), and that was not a goal race. It was a reason to keep the long run alive through the holiday hangover.

## Why this note is here

Because every year has a January, and every January feels like starting over, and the temptation is to make it dramatic. Big goals, big block, big first week. We have watched that fail in other people and in ourselves.

The version that works is smaller. Get the alarm back. Get the first easy week done. Let the five pounds take care of themselves once the routine does. The season is long. January is for finding the groove, not for proving anything.
