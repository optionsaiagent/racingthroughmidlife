The Honolulu Marathon was December 10, 2023. This is the note about the week after it, which is a week nobody films because nothing happens in it. That is the point.

## What we did

Walked. Swam easy, mostly floating with intent. Slept more than usual and did not apologize for it. Ate normally, which after a marathon means more than normal for a few days and then it settles. Did not run.

The urge to run a few days after a marathon is real. The legs feel better than expected by Wednesday and the brain says a short shakeout would be fine. At thirty, maybe. At fifty-one, the shakeout on Wednesday is what makes the first real run in January feel like it is being done on someone else's legs.

## Why the whole week

A marathon is a big withdrawal. The [season is funded with sleep](/lessons/sleep-funds-the-season), and a marathon spends most of the balance in one morning. The week after is not a reward for finishing. It is the deposit that makes the next season possible.

We plan it in advance. The week after the marathon is on the calendar as rest before the marathon is on the calendar as a race. That way it cannot be argued with in the moment by a version of us that feels surprisingly fine on Thursday.

## What comes next

January. The [five pounds and the groove](/notes/january-the-five-pounds-and-the-groove), same as the year before. Then a New Year's Day 10K in Hawaii Kai, because the calendar here does not give you much of a break, and a trail run out to Kaena Point. Then the decision about the full distance, which was already being talked about in the car on the way home from the finish.

But that is next year. This week was for sitting down.
