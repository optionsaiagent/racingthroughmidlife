This is not a shoe review. We are not going to tell you about stack height or carbon plates, because we do not race in anything we have not trained in and we do not name anything we have not raced in.

Here is what Jay wore and why.

## May 2023

Two pairs in the rotation that month. Asics Gel Nimbus 24 for the training weeks. New Balance Fresh Foam X 1080 v12 for the two races, Hibiscus Half at the end of May and Honu the week after.

The 1080s got the races because they had already done the long runs. Not because of a spec. Because of a Saturday morning in April when they went past ten miles without an argument from a calf.

## The rule

Put the race shoe on a long run before the race. At least one. Preferably the longest one in the block.

A new shoe has a drop, a stack, a fit, and an opinion about your foot, and your calf has not met any of those yet. Race day is not the introduction. The long run is. If the long run goes fine, the shoe gets the bib. If it does not, you found out on a Saturday with nothing on the line.

## Why we bother writing this

Because the internet is full of people buying a race shoe the week of the race, and at fifty a calf that disagrees with a shoe at mile ten is not a nuisance, it is the next three weeks of the season.

Two pairs. One trained, one raced. Nothing new in the box on race morning.
