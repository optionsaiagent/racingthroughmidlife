It is a strange thing to explain to someone on the mainland: we live in Honolulu and we ride a bike in a garage staring at a screen. The weather outside is the weather people fly here for.

Here is why it won.

## The problems it solved

**The dark.** The riding window is dawn. Dawn is dark. Riding in traffic in the dark on an island where the roads were not built with a shoulder in mind is a risk we take on the weekend when there is time to pick the route, and not one we take on a Tuesday at 5 a.m.

**The clock.** A weekday ride outside is the ride plus getting to a road worth riding plus getting home. On the trainer, an hour is an hour. At fifty with a day job, the hour that is actually an hour is the one that happens.

**The heat.** The trainer has a fan. The fan does not have a dew point. A hard interval session in the garage is a hard interval session, not a hard interval session plus a heat problem. The [zone 2 note](/notes/zone-2-in-a-sauna) explains why that matters.

**The honesty.** The trainer does not have a downhill. Every minute is a minute of pedaling. It is a harder hour than the same hour outside, and it is a measured one.

## How it went

Late October 2023 was the first week: a few rides, a race or two on the app, and the surprise that a virtual race hurts exactly as much as a real one. By November we were hooked. Zwift Academy in November. The Fondo series in December. It became the weekday bike, full stop.

The same week as the first trainer rides, Jay rode over the Pali Highway for the first time, outside, on a real road with a real climb. That is the balance: the trainer for the weekdays and the specific efforts, the island for the weekends and the long ones. The trainer did not replace the road. It replaced the weekday rides that were not happening.

## What we would tell a friend

If your weekday rides are not happening because of dark, traffic, heat, or time, a trainer fixes all four. It does not fix motivation, and it does not make you enjoy staring at a wall, but the app helps more than we expected and the hour is a real hour.

It is not cheating. It is the only way we found to ride four days a week and still have a job.
