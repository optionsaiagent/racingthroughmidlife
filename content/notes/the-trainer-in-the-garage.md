It's a strange thing to explain to somebody on the mainland: we live in Honolulu and we ride a bike in the garage staring at a screen, while the weather outside is the weather people fly here for. We didn't expect it to stick either, but it solved four problems we'd been pretending weren't problems.

## What it fixed

The first was the dark. The riding window is dawn, and dawn is dark, and riding in traffic in the dark on an island where the roads weren't built with a shoulder in mind is a risk we take on the weekend, when there's time to pick the route, and not on a Tuesday at 5 a.m.

The second was the clock. A weekday ride outside is the ride plus getting to a road worth riding plus getting home, while on the trainer an hour is an hour. At fifty with a day job, that's the difference between the ride happening and not happening.

The third was the heat. The trainer's got a fan, and with the fan running a hard interval session in the garage is a hard interval session, not a hard interval session plus a heat problem. The [zone 2 note](/notes/zone-2-in-a-sauna) gets into why that matters more than it sounds like it should.

The fourth was honesty. The trainer doesn't have a downhill, so every minute is a minute of pedaling, which makes it a harder hour than the same hour outside and a measured one.

## How it went

Late October 2023 was the first week: a few rides, a race or two on the app, and the surprise that a virtual race hurts exactly as much as a real one. By November we were hooked, with Zwift Academy in November and the Fondo series in December, and somewhere in there it became the weekday bike, full stop.

The same week as the first trainer rides, I rode over the Pali Highway for the first time, outside, on a real road with a real climb, and that's the balance we've kept since: trainer for the weekdays and the specific efforts, island for the weekends and the long ones. The trainer didn't replace the road, it replaced the weekday rides that weren't happening.

If your weekday rides aren't happening because of dark, traffic, heat, or time, a trainer fixes all four. It doesn't fix motivation, and it won't make you enjoy staring at a wall, but the app helped more than we expected and the hour is a real hour. It's the only way we found to ride four days a week and still have jobs.
