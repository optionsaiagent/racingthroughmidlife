The first version of this site was three race reports, three notes, three lessons, and a page that linked to two videos. It did the job of existing. It did not do the job of being the written log.

So we rebuilt it. Same address, same rules, more of the actual record.

## What is different

Every race since 2014 has a page now, with the video where there is one and an honest blank where there is no official time. The [Oahu calendar](/calendar) is a page, month by month, because the local calendar is the backbone of how we plan a year and nobody had written it down from the age-grouper side. There is a [start here](/start-here) page for the person who is 48 and looking at the finisher photos and wondering. The field notes and the lessons are twice as long and there are more of them.

## What a Sunday looks like from here

One note a week, if we did the work. That is the deal we are making with the page. It will not be long. It will say what the week actually looked like, what race is next, and whatever we learned that would survive being told to a friend at dinner. When there is a race, there is a race report, and it goes up with the video.

If a Sunday goes by without a note, it is because the week did not have anything worth writing down, or because we were tired. Both are allowed. The log is written in pencil, like the plan.

## The rules that did not change

No invented results. No coaching, no clinic, no medical advice. No funnel, for anything. Gear gets named only if it got raced in. We do not sell electrolytes.

Two Honolulu age-groupers. Swim, bike, run, and the week around it. Same as before, written down properly this time.
