The first version of this site was three race reports, three notes, three lessons, and a page that linked to two videos. It did the job of existing. It didn't do the job of being the written log.

So we rebuilt it. Same address, same rules, a lot more of the actual record.

## What's different

Every race since 2014 has a page now, with the video where there is one and an honest blank where there's no official time. The [Oahu calendar](/calendar) is a page, month by month, because the local calendar is the backbone of how we plan a year and nobody had written it down from the age-grouper's side. There's a [start here](/start-here) page for the person who's 48 and looking at the finisher photos and wondering. The field notes and the lessons are twice as long and there are more of them.

## What a Sunday looks like from here

One note a week, if we did the work. That's the deal we're making with the page. It won't be long. It'll say what the week actually looked like, what race is next, and whatever we learned that would survive being told to a friend over dinner. When there's a race, there's a race report, and it goes up with the video.

If a Sunday goes by without a note, it's because the week didn't have anything worth writing down, or because we were tired. Both are allowed. The log's in pencil, same as the plan.

## What didn't change

No made-up results. No coaching, no clinic, no medical advice. No funnel, for anything. Gear only gets named if it got raced in. We don't sell electrolytes.

Two Honolulu age-groupers. Swim, bike, run, and the week around it. Same as before, written down properly this time.
