Every race-nutrition article on the internet seems to end with a link to buy something. This one doesn't, because we don't sell anything and we're not going to recommend a brand we haven't raced on. What we can tell you is how the list gets made, because the list is the only part we're sure about.

## The rule

Nothing goes in a race pocket that hasn't already been in a training pocket on a hot day for at least three hours.

That's the whole method, and it isn't sophisticated. It works because the way race nutrition usually fails isn't that a bar has the wrong ratio of something. It's that the bar turned into a brick in the heat and your stomach refused it at mile 70. You find that out on a Saturday, or you find it out in the lava.

## How the list gets made

Every long ride and every brick is an audition. Whatever's in the pockets at the start gets graded at the end on two questions: did it get eaten, and did it stay eaten. The stuff that comes home still in the pocket didn't get eaten, which means it didn't work, which means it's off the list. The stuff that got eaten and caused a problem is off the list too. The stuff that got eaten and caused nothing is on the list, and by the end of a block that list is short. It's also proven, and that's the one that goes to the race.

## What humidity does to the list

Chocolate's a liquid here by 7 a.m. Anything with a texture that depends on being cool becomes something else, and a gel that was fine on a cool morning is a different substance after two hours in a jersey pocket in August. The heat changes the food, and the only way to know how is to carry it in the heat and find out.

Salt matters more here than the packaging lets on. Sweat here is heavy and salty, and a long day without replacing it ends badly. We usually handle that with real food and with whatever's in the bottles that week. We're not going to tell you what to buy. We'll tell you to test it in the heat first.

## The 112-mile audition

The [Dick Evans](/races/dick-evans-112-2023), 112 miles around Oahu in August, was the longest audition of 2023. Everything on the list for the full distance had to survive that day first. Some of it did. The stuff that didn't isn't on the list anymore.
