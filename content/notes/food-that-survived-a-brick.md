Every race-fueling article on the internet ends with a link to buy something. This one does not, because we do not sell anything and we are not going to recommend a brand we have not raced on.

What we can tell you is how the list gets made.

## The rule

Nothing goes in a race pocket that has not already been in a training pocket on a hot day for at least three hours.

That is the whole method. It is not sophisticated. It works because the failure mode of race nutrition is not "this bar has the wrong ratio." It is "this bar turned into a brick in the heat and my stomach refused it at mile 70." You find that out on a Saturday, or you find it out in the lava.

## How the list gets made

Every long ride and every brick is an audition. Whatever is in the pockets at the start gets evaluated at the end by two questions: did it get eaten, and did it stay eaten. The stuff that comes home still in the pocket did not get eaten, which means it did not work, which means it is off the list. The stuff that got eaten and caused a problem is off the list. The stuff that got eaten and caused nothing is on the list.

By the end of a block the list is short. It is also proven. That is the list that goes to the race.

## What humidity does to the list

Chocolate is a liquid here by 7 a.m. Anything with a texture that depends on being cool becomes something else. Gels that were fine on a cool morning are a different substance after two hours in a jersey pocket in August. The heat changes the food, and the only way to know how is to carry it in the heat.

Salt matters more here than the packaging suggests. Sweat here is heavy and salty, and a long day without replacing it ends badly. We do that with real food and with what is in the bottles. We are not going to tell you what to buy. We will tell you to test it in the heat first.

## The 112-mile audition

The [Dick Evans](/races/dick-evans-112-2023), 112 miles around Oahu in August, was the longest audition of 2023. Everything on the list for the full distance had to survive that day first. Some of it did. The ones that did not are not on the list anymore. That is how the list works.
