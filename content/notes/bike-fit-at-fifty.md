A bike fit is an hour or two with someone who knows what they are doing, adjusting the bike to your body instead of the other way around. Saddle height, reach, cleat position, the angle of things you did not know had angles.

Michelle had hers in the third week of August 2023, in the middle of a season that had a 112-mile ride and the Marathon Readiness Series in it. It should have happened a year earlier.

## Why it matters more now

A body at fifty has opinions. A back that tolerated a bad position at thirty does not anymore. A knee that tracks slightly wrong on every pedal stroke will make that known somewhere around mile forty of a long ride, and it will keep making it known for the rest of the week.

A fit is not about speed. It is about being able to ride long without paying for it in ways that show up on the run, or in the next week's training, or in a rib that was already unhappy.

## What changed

We are not going to list saddle millimeters. What we will say is that the long rides after the fit were different rides. Less shifting around on the saddle. Less of the hand-numbness that had been treated as normal. A position that could be held for hours, which is the entire job of a position when the ride is 100 miles.

## The order we would recommend

Fit first, bike second. A fit on an old bike beats no fit on a new one. Jay's [tri bike arrived in July](/lessons/the-bike-came-after-the-race), and the honest version of that story is that both of us should have paid for a fit before either of us paid for a frame.

If you are starting this after 45 and you are going to spend money on one thing for the bike, spend it on the person who adjusts the bike to you.
